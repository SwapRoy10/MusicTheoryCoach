## Recommended varied example

`varied-example.musicxml`, generated by `make_varied.py`, is also an original CC0 exercise with implementation-level review, not independent musician review. Use `varied-assignment.json`, which does not prescribe a progression.

| Location | S | A | T | B | Sounding harmony |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| m. 1, beat 1 | G4 | E4 | C4 | C3 | I |
| m. 2, beat 1 | A4 | F4 | D4 | F3 | ii6 |
| m. 2, beat 3 | A4 | D4 | C4 | F3 | ii6/5 |
| m. 3, beat 1 | G4 | D4 | B3 | G3 | V |
| m. 4, beat 1 | G4 | E4 | C4 | C3 | I |

The first transition has S-T parallel fifths (C4/G4 to D4/A4). Changing measure-2 beat-1 tenor to C4 removes the parallel and changes that chord to IV. The seventh C4 in measure 2 beat 3 resolves down to B3 in measure 3; that B then resolves up to C4 in measure 4. Measure 3 differs from both tonic measures. ## Original regression fixtures

`valid.musicxml` and `deliberate-errors.musicxml` were authored for this project; dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. No third-party composition is embedded. Source: `make_examples.py`. Review status: mechanically tested and manually reasoned during implementation; **not independently musician-reviewed and not trusted training data**.

| Measure | Harmony | S | A | T | B |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C major, root position | G4 | E4 | C4 | C3 |
| 2 | F major, root position | A4 | F4 | C4 | F3 |
| 3 | C major, root position | G4 | E4 | C4 | C3 |
| 4 | C major, root position | G4 | E4 | C4 | C3 |

All notes are whole notes on beat 1. The tenor holds C4 throughout, preventing parallels with the moving soprano. S–A intervals are thirds; A–T are thirds/fourths; bass motion does not produce parallel fifths/octaves with moving upper voices. The final repetition is stationary, not a parallel.

The error fixture changes only measure 2 tenor to D4. S–T moves C4/G4 → D4/A4 → C4/G4: two same-direction perfect-fifth transitions. D is not a member of the supplied F–A–C triad. Reverting this tenor note restores the valid fixture. The error chord also omits C, producing an advisory incompleteness finding.

The actual sounding measure-2 chord is D-F-A over F, or ii6. It is only a membership error because `assignment.json` explicitly prescribes IV; without that requirement it is a valid triad with voice-leading problems. It is not ii7 because C is absent.

Regenerate from the repository root with `python -m examples.make_examples`. The fixtures and their variations belong in the same future dataset split; they are development tests, not a held-out quality measurement.
