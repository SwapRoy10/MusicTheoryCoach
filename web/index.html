What the review covers

Chorales. Four voices in a major or minor key and 4/4, as block chords or as two voices on each staff with their own rhythms: passing tones, neighbors, suspensions and other nonchord tones are labeled, and applied chords, mixture, the Neapolitan and augmented sixths are recognized: parallel and direct intervals, ranges, spacing, overlap, tendency tones, Roman numerals with inversions, cadences, and the contrapuntal paradigms from the reference library.

Species counterpoint. A cantus firmus in whole notes and one counterpoint above or below it, first through fifth species: consonance and dissonance treatment, motion into perfect intervals, melodic rules, suspensions and cadences, each finding citing the rule and its sources.

Hear it and fix it. Play the score with the sounding notes lit, listen to any single finding, and ask for a fix: each one is a single-note change that the reviewer has rechecked, and you can apply and undo it.

Compose. Species counterpoint, a new chorale phrase, or four parts around your own melody or bass.

Practice. Get an exercise (a cantus firmus, a melody or a bass), write your answer on the score in the browser, and check it.
